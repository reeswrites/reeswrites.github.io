I just got a Sonos and love it. I haven’t cast many albums to it so far, I’ve actually been really into using it for radio.

2. Sonos Radio Sunset Fuzz Station

3. WAMU 88.5 DC NPR Station

## YouTube

[Here's what I’m listening to right now: — Derrick Gee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FMfPYdts_yI)

I love radio shows so it was nice to hear someone speak about music so lovingly and with deep knowledge and appreciation. This was mostly background music for me, but I found myself bopping to the grooves quite often.

[100 gecs | Boiler Room: Los Angeles — Boiler Room](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8NWHnWbpxmc)

Best described by one of the top comments: “this set is like having a bunch of tabs open playing audio but you can’t figure out which sound is coming from which”.

[Jazz piano into Summer ⛱️ — Jazz Jane 정의주](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-DOD0o0I570)

I’ve been listening to so much Jazz on YouTube recently, but this was someone playing it instead of a playlist/livestream!

## Spotify

[In The Flesh — Ecco2k](https://open.spotify.com/track/0UGwYOxzR4Gra267AmQPdR)

Futuristic music, if you told me this is what people were listening to in 2049, I would believe you. A rediscovery for me. I’ve always liked Bladee more within Drain Gang, but Ecco is such a good and interesting musician that I wish he dropped more.

[Somewhere City — Origami Angel](https://open.spotify.com/album/4h2ME3sBJVi1X3KTwLJXnn)

Emo pop/midwest emo record that I just discovered this week. I was looking around for new albums/artists in lanes that I already am in, or just slightly outside of it to slowly expand my tastes.

[Nobody — Chief Keef](https://open.spotify.com/album/5J5iwGQ7Vd1Uu4pxKSPMln)

4NEM and Dirty Nachos are probably my favorite Chief Keef projects, both of them of his late work, so when TikTok randomly showed me an old Keef song I followed it and listened to a bunch of his old projects and found some nice songs. I don’t love the tapes in their totality usually, but I can always find one or two bangers.

## SoundCloud

[oryx - side eye *extended intro* prod oryx and me — Pawl4k](https://soundcloud.com/prodpawl4k/oryx-side-eye-extended-intro)

Discovered [oryx](https://soundcloud.com/hateoryx) at a [concert in DC at Union Stage](https://www.unionstagepresents.com/shows/hard-reset-vol-1-alice-gas-blackwinterwells-ft-hateoryx-down-special-fryenation-23-jul), so I’ve been following his releases ever since

[zombie prod comanati — Senses](https://soundcloud.com/7senss/zombie-prod-comanati)

Senses is digicore vocaloid music that keeps on evolving!

[MIDWXST - TWIN SISTERS \[PROD : Deadmarni\] — Deadmarni](https://soundcloud.com/deadmarni/midwxst-twin-sisters-prod-deadmarni-1)

Hyperpop adjacent rapper Midwxst doesn’t always do it for me, but this A/B side release from him was hitting

[DEADER8LIVE x DEATHNOTES - RIPKEITH (extra prod xtc, x4, cabe) — dead8ralive](https://soundcloud.com/deaderalive/keith)

Loved their Kid Trash project, and just learned that they discontinued it and have been releasing under a new name now.
