A garden is not a blog. These are notes I am still growing — published earlier and rougher than my posts, revised in place rather than fixed to the day I wrote them, and sorted by how far along they are rather than by date.

Some notes contain passages written by an AI assistant. Those are always marked and attributed inline, and flagged 🤖 in the lists below.

The garden has its own feed, separate from the main one, so you can follow the finished writing and the growing notes independently.
